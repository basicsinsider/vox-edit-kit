## Notes for editors
- **UN chamber (N01)**: the 1962 Stevenson/Cuba photo is a high-angle real SC chamber with the horseshoe table and the Krohg mural — crop to the table for the veto box; don't let the missile boards read as a story beat (blur/crop the easels).
- **GA hall (N02)** is a 2006 photo (hall unchanged since 1952); grade down / mask the two video screens beside the emblem. No ≥3000 px 1950s–60s GA photo was reachable (UN Photo site is behind a JS bot challenge).
- **Mao 1949 proclamation (N08)** only exists at 771 px on Commons — use as a panel/inset (Vox layered), not full frame.
- **Chiang (N30)**: three ≥2500 px options (cape+map 4788×5878 → 2.5D: cut-out in `cutouts/`, the wall map of China is the backdrop; 1945 Navy portrait 2845×3645; dress-uniform portrait 2517×3331).
- **1895 print (N15)** is a single-sheet colour print of the Sankakuyū battle (stylised combat; one fallen figure) — keep the push on the officer/landscape.
- **Nanking parade 1937** (N17 option) is the Japanese entry parade into Nanking — non-graphic but loaded; prefer the Shanghai 1937 naval-troops photo or footage unless the editor wants the marching column.
- **Land reform**: the 1960s poster and booklet are museum-case photos (CC BY-SA, credit) — cropped to the object; the 1963 10th-anniversary stamp is the clean colour option.
- **Taiwan Cement share certificate (N24)** is angled under glass (CC BY-SA) — use as a tilted prop on the desk; Taiwan Cement was one of the four state firms whose shares compensated landlords.
- **Hyperinflation (N21)**: real 1949 Bank of Taiwan ONE-MILLION cheque + 1946 500 note — small scans, props at ≤ native size.
- **USAAF Formosa raids (N19)**: four 4500 px NARA print cards (with typed captions and punch holes — nice archival frame; crop inside the photo for a clean shot).
- Rejected: Academia Historica photo of Chen Cheng showing land-reform results (國史館 watermark); Truman Library 1945 Keelung photos (blurred, 800 px); Mossadegh SC 1951 (Flushing Meadows, not the NY chamber); fisheye GA photo.
