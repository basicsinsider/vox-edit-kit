# assets/intro/papers — vintage grid / ruled paper grounds (asset gatherer C, 2026-10-31)

All REAL scans (never vector-drawn). Prepared with `tools/prep_texture.py` (output dir redirected here):
`NAME.jpg` = 1920 px-wide working copy (**upscaled** when the scan is smaller), `NAME_hires.jpg` = native scan resolution,
`NAME_norm(_hires).jpg` = desaturated, median at 50 % grey → overlay / soft-light blend. Originals = paper-coloured → multiply
or use as a background plate. Grade toward ink-grey (≤ 30 % saturation) so the palette stays ours.

## K+E Graph Sheets Catalog 1960 — archive.org `kecatalog`
Source: https://archive.org/details/kecatalog (Keuffel & Esser Co., "Graph Sheets — cross section and profile papers, cloths and
films", 1960; 66 pages, `kecatalog_jp2.zip`, 2448×3168 per page). Page numbers below = jp2 index `kecatalog_NNNN.jp2`.

**Licence reasoning:** the catalogue carries a notice on its title verso (jp2 0002): *"Copyright 1927, 1936, 1943, 1944, 1949, 1954,
1960 by Keuffel & Esser Co. — Printed in U.S.A."*. A 1960 US publication stays protected only if renewed in 1987–88; we did not
find/verify a renewal (not checked — copyright.gov renewal search unavailable here), so treat the *catalogue text/layout* as possibly
in copyright. The **graph-sheet rulings themselves are blank forms** (graph paper, ruled ledger sheets) that "are designed for
recording information and do not in themselves convey information" — not copyrightable under US law (37 CFR 202.1(c)). Therefore:
the swatch crops (rulings only, no text) are used as paper grounds; the full-page scans (with K+E text and logo) are **props only,
blurred / partial, never a featured readable page and never the K+E logo** — or skip them if the lead prefers zero risk.
