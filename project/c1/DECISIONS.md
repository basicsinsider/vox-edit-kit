# Decisions on the c1 open questions (planner defaults; Krish / lead may override)

1. **Palette for the Silicon Valley story** — `--west` vermilion = the West / California / Stanford / Terman's Silicon Valley /
   HP; `--east` navy (sky on night) = the East coast, MIT, Harvard, Bell Labs, GE, Eli Lilly; yellow = highlighter + one key
   number per scene (12, 90 %, 1891, $100, 25, 800). Celadon unused. People stay neutral sepia/ink — **no red portrait
   duotones** (red will mean Morris Chang/TSMC once the Taiwan story returns), vermilion only for Stanford/West tags, bars,
   routes and fills.
2. **No face cam** anywhere in c1 (§10.9). Direct-address asides ("let me paint you a picture", "like my brother", "Did they have
   Pentagon back then?") get literal graphics, never the narrator.
3. **"born in the 1900s"** — on screen `1900` (his own explanation "a very lucky year… like being born in 2000" fixes the meaning);
   not a fact-check.
4. **"a very, very powerful, powerful company"** (= family) — never caption "company"; `very powerful` over the father-and-son diagram.
5. **"in 1980, 1891"** — show only `1891` (self-correction).
6. **"the 20s"** — on screen `the 20s` (his words), not "1920s".
7. **Stanford's business school** — kicker = his words `business graduate program`; the real name *Stanford Graduate School of
   Business* only as a small object label on the building/bulletin.
8. **§10.7 painting-to-print** is used twice: the cold-open Terman quote (C1S001–003: orchard painting → aerial → column with
   Terman photo) and Hoover's argument (C1S045–046: Greene's 1956 painted portrait → column, pull-quote underlined word by word).
   Columns are typeset recreations — no real mastheads/dates.
9. **The quote is unattributed until 14.32** ("Fred") — no name/portrait in C1S001–002.
10. **Twitter-joke aside (176.67–208.43)** — kept literal and quick: typeset tweet card (generic avatar, no real handle, no words
    he didn't say), Puck-style colour cartoon of an idle man, a light switch, a trading floor, a real vintage HELP WANTED
    classified column. No real people.
11. **Lou Henry Hoover** — narrator says only "his wife": on screen `his wife`; her name may appear as a tiny photo caption.
12. **Ray Lyman Wilbur & other "people he trusted"** — portrait circles without names (VO names nobody).
13. **12 trillion-dollar companies** — reuse the dated list (companiesmarketcap.com, 2026-10-29, `example-docs/analysis/BEATS.md` §6)
    with the source line; the narrated counts (12 / 11 / Eli Lilly) win if the market moved.
14. **Charts without narrated data** (Berkeley vs Stanford ranking, ten-year line, board seats, money flows, fields bars) —
    shapes only, no axis values, no invented ranks or names. The 25-company list shows 25 unnamed scribbled rows.
15. **800 people isotype** — 80 icons, legend "1 icon = 10 people" (a unit label, not a new claim).
16. **"Did they have Pentagon back then?"** — the Pentagon with a hand-drawn `?`; no date, no answer.
17. **Disney** — *Fantasia* (1940) poster as the Disney image (fair use, credited); the old "8 oscillators" fact is not shown
    (not narrated).
18. **Coin flip** — a real 1930s US coin (PD Mint imagery) flipped in CSS 3D; the coin's landing reveals HEWLETT-PACKARD order
    (no claim of which side won).
19. **Screens** — era-true: 1950s TV (classroom test), 1940s oscilloscope (oscillator), WWII radar PPI scope (radar). Not the four
    museum TVs, not Tatung/Trinitron (1980s Taiwan sets belong to the Taiwan chunks).
20. **2.5D** — three moments: Main Quad 1891 (approved), 1920 Stanford group with young Terman, the HP garage (approved s10).
21. **Copyrighted images** (Terman Stanford News photos, HP press images, Fantasia poster, war front pages, trading floor) — fair
    use: logged `© owner, fair use` + URL, 13 px credit on screen. Getty/AP not downloaded.
22. **Maps** — the approved s02 US system re-timed, basemap upgraded from `assets/maps/kit/`; vintage scans (Rand McNally 1920,
    pictorial Peninsula map) for the atlas looks. No flat grey/beige relief.
23. **Transitions flagged "planner rhythm fix"** in EDIT_NOTES may be swapped by the editor for any other type that doesn't repeat
    its neighbours.
24. **Hand-off** — c1 opens out of the intro's bone flash; c1 ends on a continuing drift (no fade) for c2 to pick up.
