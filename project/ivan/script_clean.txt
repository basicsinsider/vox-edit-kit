But how do you know who you can actually trust? The person passing information to Ukraine could be someone you've worked with for years, someone you see every day without suspecting a thing. And strangely enough, Ukraine has to worry about the same problem. Imagine spending months working with a Russian who claims to hate Putin. Everything he's told you seems to check out, so you begin to trust him. But what if he's actually been reporting back to Moscow all along? Neither side can ever be completely sure who they're dealing with. And even for those Russians who genuinely are working against Putin, there's another question nobody can answer. Will any of this actually bring about the change they're hoping for?
Ivan certainly doesn't know. He could be arrested tomorrow, and the war could continue for years. But when he spoke to Mamoń about the future, he made one thing clear: "Putin won't live forever." And he's right. Sooner or later, Russia will have to confront the question of what comes after him. Whether that brings the change Ivan hopes for is another matter entirely. But for now, he and others like him have decided they're willing to risk everything for a future they may never get to see.
