## 2. Timeline
- 1984 Credicentro founded (Víctor Hugo Vázquez). 2015 Miguel Vázquez's "digital revolution" era (group also buys rural estates). 2018–19 Peña buys Credicentro shares (finance minister 2015–17).
- 2021 Grupo Vázquez buys Financiera El Comercio → renamed **ueno** (Dec 2021); Carlos Carvallo chairman (from mid-2021).
- 31 Jul 2023 president-elect Peña names Carvallo for the BCP; 15 Aug 2023 Peña inaugurated; 7 Sep 2023 Carvallo sworn in at the BCP. Oct–Dec 2023 ueno becomes a bank.
- 20 Jan 2024 ueno–Visión merger announced; Jun 2024 completed. Jul 2024 Moody's Baa3 (first investment grade).
- 2024–25 IPS deposits flow into ueno. 22 Mar 2025 Peña's Ueno Holding shares redeemed ("sold March 2025"). Apr 2025 ABC "aplastantes nexos" stories.
- Mar 2026 ueno US$350 M bond. 15 Jun 2026 Vinanzas buys US$74.4 M loan portfolio. 4 Jul 2026 France 1–0 Paraguay; Amarilla tweet. Jul 2026 ABC on IPS funds over the 10 % cap. 14 Sep 2026 ABC: Senate presses BCP on US$40 M maneuver.
- **2 Nov 2026** BCP board votes unanimously (solvency 5.56 % vs 12 % required — < half the minimum); **3 Nov** announced (early morning); app/cards frozen; Carvallo resigns. **4 Nov** 8 directors detained / 27 charged; protests day 2 at Congress, tear gas. **4–5 Nov** emergency law (deposit-guarantee advance) promulgated; first payout **up to G. 10,000,000 ≈ US$1,675–1,700** per saver (the narrator's "~$1,500 a month"); ~**97 % of clients fully covered** (guarantee up to 75 minimum wages ≈ US$38,000). **5 Nov** impeachment libel (35 signatures) vs Peña + Alliana; Peña's resignation video; not accepted. **6 Nov** Peña's 72-h extension letter rejected; Senate removes Peña (44 votes) → Pedro Alliana sworn in (term to Aug 2028) → Peña detained at home that night; X clip of crowd at Congress (6 Nov). ~5–6 Nov "tax the poor" photo. **7 Nov** Peña at the Fiscalía, then held at the Regimiento Escolta Presidencial; Carvallo detained (Agrupación Especializada, 15 days); asset freezes on Peña + Carvallo; Itaipú files criminal complaint vs ueno.
