# Decisions for chunk c4 (planner defaults; Krish / lead may override)

1. **Palette mapping for c4** — celadon = Taiwan / ROC (fills, rules, tabs; never text colour); **red = CCP / PRC** (TSMC and
   Morris Chang are absent from c4; c1 already re-mapped red for its own act, so a stated per-act mapping is the practice);
   US, Japan, UN, USSR/Warsaw Pact, and Mao / Chiang *as persons* = neutral ink/bone/sepia; navy/blue unused (UMC/Tsao);
   yellow = highlighter + one key number per scene. **Lead: align with the c3 and c5 planners** so the CCP/ROC colours do not
   flip between chunks.
2. **Scenes = beats (104 / 104)** — one scene per beat keeps the plan auditable; beats that share a layout are marked
   `cont.` (same layout, camera moves on) rather than merged.
3. **No face cam** anywhere in c4; direct-address lines get the visual of their own sentence (BEATS §5).
4. **The "seismic event" is never named** (it is a teaser: seismograph spike + "the 70s" tab). No years beyond the spoken ones.
5. **"in like 15, 2"** (517.29–519.69) — unclear ASR; no years on screen in C4S090 until Krish confirms.
6. **NTU slogan** — on screen exactly his words: "Come, come to NTU" / "Go, go, go to America"; the original Chinese
   (來來來，來台大；去去去，去美國) may appear small as typographic texture on the pennant/newspaper (decorative, not a caption).
   Built with §10.7 painting-to-print (it is a quote).
7. **Real documents keep their own words** — the UN draft resolution (C4S009), newspaper clippings (C4S011), share
   certificate (C4S054), magazine cover (C4S089) may show their printed text; our own overlay text stays his words only.
   Recreations (diplomatic cable, newspaper column, deed fallback) use no real mastheads, seals or signatures.
8. **Sensitive history is non-graphic** — Japanese army in China (C4S030–031), colonial police (C4S032), bombing (C4S042),
   White Terror (C4S063–065): marching columns, buildings, prison corridors; no bodies, no executions; no glow, sombre sound.
9. **Charts without narrated data** (votes falling, land/people/resources, yield, staircase, bigger impact, population) are
   **shapes only** — no axis values, no invented numbers. Printed numbers are only the spoken ones: 5, 6, 11, 10, 1.5 billion,
   40 years, 1949, 1890s, 1940, '50s–'65, late 60s.
10. **"Between 50s to 65"** → on screen `'50s` and `'65` (his words, typographic).
11. **Chiang Kai-shek before his name** (C4S021 with Eisenhower, 1960) — shown, never labelled until "Chiang" @507.37.
12. **Kaohsiung** is never named by the narrator — the export-processing-zone images are the true ones (Kaohsiung EPZ, 1966);
    only a 13 px photo credit/caption may say where it is.
13. **Hyperinflation banknotes** — real 1949 notes; no exchange-rate numbers on our overlays.
14. **2.5D at four moments**: GA hall (C4S005), 1950s factory (C4S056), Chiang Kai-shek (C4S088), export processing zone
    (C4S099) — the places/people that most deserve depth; each needs a ≥ 3000 px source (fallback: #16 plate with slow push).
15. **Map kit missing** — until `assets/maps/kit/` lands, editors may use `assets/intro/maps/*` at native resolution as cards
    or ghosts, never as flat full-frame relief (§10.9); the lead re-checks map scenes once the kit exists.
16. **Image reuse** — allowed when needed (§10.9): Presidential Office footage twice (different sections), Pacific map twice,
    Japanese-era cards reused small in the hand-over folder (C4S038).
17. **Hold rule** — each scene's key animation completes by end − 0.5 s; words spoken in the last 0.5 s use a ≤ 0.25 s entrance.
18. **Segments**: c4s01 0.00–199.14 (C4S001–036), c4s02 199.14–399.37 (C4S037–069), c4s03 399.37–601.44 (C4S070–104).
