- The UN session is the 26th General Assembly, New York, **25 Oct 1971** (Resolution 2758). The US "important question"
  motion failed (≈ 55 for / 59 against / 15 abstain); ROC foreign minister **Chow Shu-kai** led the delegation out before
  the 2758 vote. Famous press photos: Bush (US ambassador) with his head in his hands; Tanzania's delegation (incl. Salim
  Ahmed Salim) cheering/dancing in the aisle; the ROC delegation leaving the hall. Mostly AP/UPI/UN Photo (© — fair use).
- Kissinger's second trip to Beijing ran 20–26 Oct 1971 (photos with Zhou Enlai, White House/NARA = PD).
- Japan–PRC Joint Communiqué, Beijing, 29 Sep 1972 (Tanaka Kakuei & Zhou Enlai signing photo).
- Chiang Kai-shek's message after the withdrawal is remembered as 莊敬自強，處變不驚 ("be dignified and self-strengthening;
  stay calm in adversity"). The original Chinese may appear small as decoration under the narrator's English (lead's rule) —
  from the real image or set as an image (our three fonts have no CJK glyphs); the English line = the narrator's words.
- Chiang Ching-kuo: in Moscow from 1925 (Sun Yat-sen University; Deng Xiaoping was a fellow student), held in the USSR until
  1937, worked at the Uralmash heavy-machinery plant (Sverdlovsk); ROC Premier from 1972. Chiang Kai-shek = Generalissimo.
- Sun Yun-suan (Minister of Economic Affairs 1969–78), K. T. Li (Li Kwoh-ting, Minister of Finance 1969–76), Fei Hua
  (secretary-general of the Executive Yuan), Pan Wen-yuan (RCA Laboratories, Princeton). The unnamed telecom official is most
  likely Fang Hsien-chi (Director, Telecommunication Laboratories) — **not named on screen**.
- Ten Major Construction Projects (1974–79): Sun Yat-sen Freeway, North-Link Railway, railway electrification, Taoyuan
  (Chiang Kai-shek) International Airport, Taichung Harbor, Su-ao Port, China Steel (Kaohsiung), China Shipbuilding
  (Kaohsiung), CPC petrochemical complex, Jinshan nuclear power plant. ROC stamp sets of the projects exist (colour
  illustrations — "posters as B-roll", MANUAL §10.5).
