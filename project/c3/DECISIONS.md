# Decisions on the c3 EDIT_NOTES open questions (planner defaults; Krish / lead may override)

1. **Palette mapping for c3** — vermilion = the West / Stanford / Silicon Valley / California (c1's convention; LONGFORM:
   "one accent for the West / Stanford, navy for the East — say the mapping"); navy = the US East coast (New York, Field's code);
   celadon = Taiwan, the ROC government and Pan Wen-Yuan; neutral = US/UK/UN/Japan/everyone else. TSMC/Morris Chang (red) do not
   appear in c3, so vermilion-as-West causes no clash inside this chunk. Vermilion and celadon share a frame only in the five
   bridge scenes (C3S037, 040, 042, 046, 051), with nothing else accented.
2. **CCP and the Soviet side = ink + 45° hatch**, never red. Red is TSMC's colour for the whole video; communist-red would teach
   the viewer the wrong code and would collide with Morris Chang's red in later chunks. Ink also reads as "the other side" on
   both paper and night grounds (bone outline on night maps).
3. **"Hoover Institute" → on screen `HOOVER INSTITUTION`** (the organisation's real name), like "Hsinchu Park" → "Hsinchu Science
   Park" in the intro. The narrator's generic "best institute in the world" gets no caption.
4. **Self-corrections shown as pen corrections** — "the best institute… very good, very good at least" (C3S033) and "the belief
   assumption" (C3S103): pen writes the first word, strikes it, writes the second, exactly as he says them. This shows his words
   verbatim without emphasising a slip.
5. **Garbled lines get the meaning, not the words**: "I created like Intel" → text only `down the line` + `Intel`; "behemoth in
   the cell" → `BEHEMOTH`; "If you like to get good historians" → `historians`; "he's not paying attention" (likely "now") →
   no text, visual of Taiwan watching Silicon Valley. Lead to confirm C3B045's "not/now" by ear.
6. **Political claims (no fact-checking, minimal text)** — "CCP stay out of World War II" is the narrator's claim; per Krish's
   rule we don't fact-check, and per the text-minimal rule we don't restate it typographically. The diagram (C3S063) crosses the
   US↔CCP link on "wasn't the CCP…", and the next shot shows who fought with the US (ROC/Chiang at Cairo). Same for "they're
   still the government of Taiwan" (likely "of China"): text only `temporary` / `inconvenience` / `still`.
7. **No year labels he doesn't say** — the UN expulsion (1971), Japan–ROC treaty (1952), Mutual Defense Treaty (1954),
   Ghana (1957) are shown through real dated documents/photos only; no added captions with years or vote counts. Only "1965"
   ('65) and "four years" are narrated numbers. Charts (D01, D02, D04, D05, D13) are shapes only — no axis values.
8. **Fair-use press material** (§10.9) — NYT 26 Oct 1971 front page, Central Daily News, UN Photo images, the HBO *Silicon Valley*
   still, the 1960 Fairchild founders photo (Wayne F. Miller/Magnum), Hoover Institution stills, Pan Wen-Yuan photos: used
   short, transformed (grade, halftone, crops, markups), credited on screen (13 px) and in SOURCES.md as "© owner, fair use".
   Never logged in to get them (no TimesMachine login); if a site is blocked → fetch relay (MANUAL §8.2).
9. **No face cam** anywhere in c3 (LONGFORM). Direct-address asides ("I like their videos", "I agree with it", "I think it
   did") get visuals of what the sentence is about.
10. **Pan Wen-Yuan's Chinese name** (潘文淵) — not shown by default (Latin spelling `Pan Wen-Yuan` only); Krish to say if he wants
    the characters as a small sub-label.
11. **Screens** — IBM 5154 (podcast UI), Sony Trinitron (HBO show), a projector + pull-down screen (Hoover video — a new screen
    type), Tatung (ROC government footage — a Taiwanese maker for a Taiwanese subject). No set repeats within c3; Trinitron and
    Tatung were used once each in the intro (different chunk, different content).
12. **2.5D moments (3)** — the Fairchild founders (C3S020), Hoover Tower (C3S032) and the Cairo Conference (C3S064): the three
    places where a single photo carries a whole beat and benefits from depth. If any source is < 3000 px, fall back to #16 plate +
    #10 layered panels.
13. **Maps** — the map kit (`assets/maps/kit/`, N05) is a prerequisite; until it lands editors use the named fallbacks (s02 US
    satellite, intro Pacific/Taiwan/world files) graded to the kit look — never the flat grey/beige relief Krish rejected.
14. **"Silicon Valley" HBO episode** — not named (he doesn't name it); the show's title card on a Trinitron is enough.
15. **Recognition data** (N49) and **African independence order** (N64) drive map/grid fills only (which countries turn when),
    from Wikipedia lists dated 2026-10-31; numbers are never printed.
16. **Short scenes** (C3S001 2.3 s, C3S037 2.6 s, C3S059 2.7 s, C3S102 2.9 s) may use 0.3–0.5 s text rises — the only exception to
    the 1.0–1.6 s entrance rule — so the 0.5 s hold before the cut is kept.
17. **c2/c4 hand-offs** — c3 opens on the IC die macro already moving (cut on action from c2's last frame; the c2 editor/lead
    confirms); c3 ends on the five P5 flag circles still pulling back, which c4 should continue for "All of them have a veto".
