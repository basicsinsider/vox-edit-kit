# Decisions on the c2 EDIT_NOTES open questions (planner defaults; Krish / lead may override)

1. **Palette** — settled by the lead's cross-chunk colour decision (`project/LONGFORM.md`): Silicon Valley content uses
   vermilion = the West / Stanford / Terman / Palo Alto / Stanford Industrial Park, navy (sky on night) = the East / Bell Labs /
   New Jersey / Washington. Shockley, the Traitorous Eight, Fairchild, Noyce and all other companies stay neutral (ink/bone/grey);
   location pins may be red/navy. Yellow = highlighter + one key number per scene. No celadon in c2 (Taiwan doesn't appear).
2. **Key numbers in yellow** — `8,000` (S057), `99` (S067), `10,000` (S086), `100,000` (S116). Everything else yellow is the
   highlighter or the R10-style marker route (S097).
3. **No invented data** — charts without narrated numbers (S029–030 lifespan, S055 dividends, S098 Fairchild era band, S113–114
   invention ranking, S116–118 cost) are drawn as shapes only: no axis values, no years, no rank numbers. The only printed
   numbers are the narrated ones (1947, 8,000, one fourth, 99, tens of thousands, 10,000, eight, 100,000, millions).
4. **"jack of all trade"** — not captioned; the Jack playing card carries the idiom (C2S005). `master of one` / `master of few`
   are captioned word by word (his exact words).
5. **Slips and the word search** (139.66–149.07 "groundmaking… what is it called… Nobel pre… Priest Prize") — no text until the
   correct "Nobel Prize in Physics" @149.63; the medal's focus hunt plays the search. No caption ever shows a slip.
6. **Fair-use key photos** — Traitorous Eight (Wayne Miller / Magnum, 1960), Bell Labs trio (1948), Shockley/Noyce portraits,
   Fortune 1953, Shockley Transistor ad, Bell System ads: used with a 13 px on-screen credit and "© owner, fair use" in SOURCES.md.
   Never a watermarked copy; never strip a watermark.
7. **Paintings as B-roll** — Feininger *Gelmeroda* (steeples, painting-to-print §10.7), Meissonier *Leland Stanford*, Fuseli
   *The Nightmare* (2.2 s literal gag for "a nightmare kind of boss") — they match the words said; keep *The Nightmare* short and
   clean (no gore/zoom on the incubus).
8. **Terman's phrase** — "steeples of excellence" treated as his quote (painting-to-print). The pull-quote prints only his
   words; caption `— his saying` (no name — the narrator doesn't say it there; Terman's photo follows in S010).
9. **Shockley's mother** — real photo if found; her name is never printed (narrator says only "his mom"). If no photo:
   an empty vintage frame on the desk + the Palo Alto postcard.
10. **Unnamed things stay unnamed** — Shockley's company name (we may show its real sign/building, which carries the name, but
    our overlay text says only `a company`), Fairchild's acquirer (never named or shown), the eight's individual names (only
    "eight" and "Robert Noyce / Bob Noyce" are captioned; real photos keep their own printed captions).
11. **"Stanford Industrial Park"** — his words; never relabel as "Stanford Research Park".
12. **Map era** — Palo Alto close-ups use USGS 1953/1961 quads (PD) where possible; the 1899 topo (E43) is the fallback, shown
    without a date label. Modern views (S053, S059–060, S115) use the map kit / satellite with credit.
13. **2.5D moments** — two: C2S065 Stanford Industrial Park aerial (the "land" act's hero) and C2S095 Traitorous Eight. If N43
    is too small for 2.5D, S065 becomes a 2-layer (sky/land) parallax and the second 2.5D moves to C2S089 (Shockley team).
14. **Screens** — new sets only: a 1950s TV (S085, missile newsreel) and the Tektronix 475A scope (S123). The intro's Tatung,
    Trinitron and IBM 5154 are not reused in c2.
15. **D08 telegram** unused in c2 (no line fits) → carried to c3. All other D01–D15 used as variations (EDIT_NOTES §11.2).
16. **c1 → c2 first frame** — C2S001 assumes c1 ends on Stanford over "best at everything"; the lead aligns the first 6 frames
    with c1's last scene once c1's plan exists (SEGMENTS §1 hand-off table).
17. **Asides with no object** ("not an engineer", "drop some analogies down below", "I cannot publicly explain it", "getting off
    track") — each gets a visual that serves its sentence (pen question marks over a real technical page, a pen arrow pointing
    down, an extreme zoom "insane", a whip back to the bookmarked silicon card), never an unrelated mood shot.
18. **Hold rule** — editors may move a scene's key animation earlier (structural `S()` cue) to secure the 0.5 s hold; text cues
    stay on their words.
19. **Reuse** — allowed (§10.9); planned repeats: transistor cut-out (motif, 5×), Terman 1955 card (3×), Traitorous Eight photo
    (3× incl. crops), Founding Grant (3×), professors photo (3× incl. crops), silicon lump (3×).
20. **Hsinchu/Taiwan** — not in c2; no Taiwan colour, no Taiwan imagery.
