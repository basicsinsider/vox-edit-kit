## Deviations / substitutions
- **N04 Kissinger–Zhou July 1971 photo: not found** (Commons has only the Feb 1972 Nixon-visit set) → C5S083 uses the Nixon–Zhou toast
  (Feb 1972): "US is talking with China" literally. **N61 Nixon 15 July 1971 address footage: missing** → C5S067 shows the Nixon colour
  portrait on the RCA set; C5S068 shows Kissinger (lead override) as weave "footage". **N62** → colour still N08 with weave.
- **N36 RCA 1970–71 TV**: RCA XL-100 (1970s) fetched from Commons and keyed (`assets/c5/screens/`).
- **N44 PIA 707 is 1280 px** (not 2.5D size) → C5S058 is a pseudo-depth layered night grade, not a cut-out 2.5D.
- **N56 clipping / N56b front page / N58 treaty scan**: typeset recreations (no masthead/date). The treaty page uses the real title +
  preamble text of the 1954 treaty (public text), typeset in Crimson Pro.
- **N54 clock**: SVG graphic clock. **N65 Peking map**: crop of the 1943 Milrose map from the map kit. **N14 Chiang c.1971**: the NARA cape
  photo (c4 asset; c5s01 uses the official portrait). **N12 Mao colour**: `assets/c3/photos/mao_zedong_with_cap.jpg` (colourised, PD).
- **M02/M05 globes** rendered offline (orthographic projection in numpy) — route/pin baked into the video; text in HTML.
- C5S044 "United States" label is ink t-label under the flag; C5S056 shows "2 DAYS" (spoken "two days").
- Kissinger crops for c5s03 circles: use `assets/c5/derived/c5s02/kissinger_oval_crop.jpg` or the official portrait with
  `filter:url(#…-tint-cool)` (tint #13171c → #e6e1d6) — the cool sepia duotone used here.
