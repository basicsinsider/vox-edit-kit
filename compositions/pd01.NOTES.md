## Lessons (for the lead to add to MANUAL §4.2 — I did not edit MANUAL)
- `scene()` with `tl.fromTo(autoAlpha 0→1, 0.001)` at exactly `t` renders HIDDEN on a frame that falls exactly on `t` while the outgoing
  scene's `tl.set(autoAlpha 0)` at the same `t` already fired → one black frame (seen at 23.15 and at frame 0). pd01 shows scenes with
  `tl.set(autoAlpha 1)` at `t − 0.002`, and scene 1 by `gsap.set` only.
- A CSS `filter` on (or around) a `<video>` that carries `data-color-grading` had no visible effect in snapshots, and `FX.weave`
  overwrites `style.filter` on its element: desaturate graded footage in the grading JSON (`saturation −0.7` ≈ a clear desaturation;
  −0.45 was barely visible).
- A rule such as `#x span { position: relative; padding … }` also hits a highlighter `<span id>` inside it (higher specificity than
  the bare id rule) — the highlighter collapsed to a sliver.
- `FX.markupDefs` filter on horizontal pen strokes made them vanish (known) — the sc04 sparks carry no filter.
