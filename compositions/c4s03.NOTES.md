  Wufeng Lin family (Lin Hsien-tang, 1927 — Taiwan's best-known gentry family). Bars are neutral ink-grey (mainland leaders, not CCP
  red) vs celadon (Taiwanese) — red stays PRC-only.
- C4S087: no 1950s generals line-up (N30c ❌) → still from the 1961 newsreel of ROC generals descending the Presidential Office steps
  (Chiang is in the group, unlabelled — his name comes only at 507.37).

- C4S091: no 1960s Taiwanese engineers photo (N31 ❌) → 1960s engineers at furnaces, Fairchild stock (Prelinger) — generic
  "engineers", credited.
- C4S096: no early Shenzhen SEZ photo (N33 ❌) → typographic timeline + red block (no photo card).
- C4S097: Shenzhen skyline is a modern photo (CC BY-SA 3.0) turned into a halftone strip — reads as "China's big city", no date implied.
- C4S099: EPZ factory = E09 (Nanzih EPZ, 2008 photo of an older building, CC BY-SA); duotoned so the blue (UMC colour) disappears.
- C4S103: no EPZ assembly-line footage (N32c ❌) → 1959 Formosa aluminium-factory hall + 1960 brick-works workers (true Taiwan labour).
- C4S095 pings: the map kit's East Asia crop ends at 15° N, so Penang/Bataan-type zones are represented by pings at Masan, Iri,
