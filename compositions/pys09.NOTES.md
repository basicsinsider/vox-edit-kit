## Gotchas for MANUAL (§4.2)
- A logo PNG whose alpha is a solid disc (e.g. `bcp_logo_171.png`, navy seal on white) masks NOTHING useful in CSS `mask` — you get a flat
  coloured circle. Bake a white+alpha copy where alpha = darkness (`assets/paraguay/pys09/derived/bcp_seal_alpha.png`) and tint it in CSS.
- A yellow highlighter span with `z-index:-1` inside a card that has no stacking context of its own paints behind the card's background
  (invisible): give the card `z-index:0` (or `isolation:isolate`) — the "60" calendar page needed it.
- Procedural 2.5D object (carousel): redraw the SVG canopy (front-facing cone/band segments only, shaded by sin θ) and place riders
  (x = cx + a·cos θ, y = cy + b·sin θ, scale/brightness by depth, z-index front > pole > back) from ONE proxy tween whose ease is
  `FX.choppy("none", d, 12)` → seek-safe 12 fps rotation; a second `power2.in` tween speeds the ride into the cut. Riders are absolutely
  positioned children of a static wrapper so their z-index sorts against the pole and canopy inside the camera's stacking context.
- Chalk text: a static (non-boiling) feTurbulence displacement + alpha-noise `feComposite in` filter reads as chalk grain; reveal words with
  `clip-path: inset(0 100% 0 0) → inset(0 0 0 0)` at 12 fps (handwriting wipe) — no boil, so it obeys the "rough must settle" rule.
- Commons search hit "Carousel at Coney Island.jpg" is only the sign lettering — look before using; "Coney Carousel 006.JPG" is the ride.
