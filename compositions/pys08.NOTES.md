## Gotchas for MANUAL (lead)
1. place_sound.py **asserts on unknown marker types** — check your sfx.json types against its `GAIN` table (e.g. `bar_rise`,
   `glass_crack`, `stone_slide` don't exist; use `counter_ticks`, `paper_tear`, `string_pull`/`whoosh_soft`). There is a `rewind` type and a `cork_pin` type.
2. **Word-width pen underline without measuring**: put an inline `<svg viewBox="0 0 100 20" preserveAspectRatio="none">` absolutely
   inside the word's span (left/right −4 px), path with `pathLength="1"`, `vector-effect="non-scaling-stroke"`,
   `stroke-dasharray="1 1"` and tween `stroke-dashoffset` 1 → 0 — it fits any word at render time (fonts loaded) and stays seek-safe.
3. **One scene, several windows**: a cork board used in non-adjacent scenes (sc018 / sc021 / sc023) is ONE scene element listed three
   times in the SC table (`[id, element, start, end, ground]`); its cards accumulate and the camera re-frames per window. Give the cork
   texture a big overscan (left −1500 / top −1000 / 6000×3700) so the board camera can push anywhere without showing an edge.
4. Brand logos as **CSS masks** (`mask-image:url(svg)` + `background: var(--ink)`) put every brand into our palette (ink on paper,
   bone on teal). A PNG logo on an opaque box (Crónica's yellow box) masks as a solid rectangle — derive an alpha mask from the
   letters' darkness first (PIL), as in `assets/paraguay/pys08/derived/cronica_mask.png`.
5. A segment that starts 0.05 s before an EDL join (pys08: 798.00 vs join 798.05) shows one frame of the previous take if it opens on
   the face cam — open on a graphic or start the face clip at the join.
