## Gotchas for MANUAL
- A paper **slide** out of a face-cam shot (EARLY window) hides the last 0.45 s of the face — and its lower-third text. If a word lands in
  the last ~0.6 s of the shot, use a hard cut instead (happened twice here: "2020", "four times bigger").
- Face-cam cut on an EDL join: frames of the assembled master sit on the 24 fps grid from 0, frames of the segment preview on a grid
  from SEG_START (457.40 is not frame-aligned). A boundary at `join + 0.003` is safe on both grids unless the join itself is a master
  grid frame (556.9167 = 13366/24): then end the face shot just BEFORE the join (556.91).
- 3D card flip in an org chart: give the card its own full-frame `perspective` wrapper, two faces with `backface-visibility:hidden`
  (back pre-rotated 180°), and drive x/y/rotationY from one proxy tween's `onUpdate` (arc path) — clean, seek-safe.
- `.hl` highlighter on display words works with `style="--hl:0"` inline and a GSAP tween of `"--hl"` 0→1.
